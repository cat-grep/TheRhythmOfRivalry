Two tempos — or one?

The raw data shows two clusters of tempo: around 75 BPM and around 150 BPM. But 75 is exactly half of 150. Marching band music is often written in cut time, so the same pulse can be counted either way.

    Post-game

    Methods & caveats

    Data

    • Fight song metadata and lyric tropes: FiveThirtyEight, with BTAA updates. Stadium coordinates: CFBD API.
    • The BTAA update mixes years (some schools already in their 2024 conference, others not), so conference membership was re-coded for 2019 and 2024–26.
    • Rivalries are a curated list of 33 long-running series between schools in the dataset.

    Analysis

    • Folded tempo: songs under 100 BPM are doubled, a common correction for half-time counting.
    • Musical distance: Gower distance over folded BPM (weight 2), length, year, student authorship and eight tropes.
    • Spatial statistics: Moran's I and LISA with 5-nearest-neighbour weights; Mantel test between great-circle and musical distance. All p-values come from 9,999 permutations (999 for LISA).
    • Conference differences: permutation χ² test; intervals are Wilson 95%.

    Caveats

    • 65 songs is a small sample. Several conferences have fewer than 15 schools, so a single song moves a percentage by 7+ points.
    • Eight tests on conference differences means one “significant” result is not surprising by chance alone.
    • Reproduce everything with python analysis/build_data.py.
